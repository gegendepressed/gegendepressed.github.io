  • Native Apple Mail app: The destination email account must be set up inside Apple's native Mail app on your iPhone/iPad. Third-party apps (Gmail, Outlook apps) cannot pass QuickType suggestions to Safari.
  • Primary Inbox required: If the ESP mail lands in Junk/Spam, iOS security suppresses the autofill suggestion.
  • Safari domain match: Safari URL must match the @sairaj.is-a.dev domain. (If accessing via gegendepressed.github.io, it redirects to sairaj.is-a.dev).
  • iOS Settings: Verify in iOS Settings > Passwords > Password Options (or Settings > General > AutoFill & Passwords) that AutoFill Passwords and Passkeys is ON.
  • Delete After Use feature: If iOS is set to "Delete After Use", iOS will automatically delete the OTP email from Mail as soon as you tap the QuickType autofill bar.
  • Recency: The email must arrive recently (usually within the last ~10-15 minutes).
