About

I’m a developer who likes building small things that are actually used: a social app that escaped its college‑project origins, a Reddit joke fetcher that friends quietly rely on, and a finance tracker that tries to make money feel a bit less abstract.

When I’m not glued to a screen, I’m usually with my tabla, reading sci‑fi, or dissecting a sitcom episode like it’s a technical document. I enjoy systems that feel human: clear, fast, and a little opinionated.

This site is a snapshot of that mix – not a "developer template", just a place to put things I care about.
